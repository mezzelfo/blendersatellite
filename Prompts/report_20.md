## B. Variants

Settings:
- **REF:** the original tiles.
- **GRID:** `terrain_to_render.bin_to_spacing` (imported) on the 1 m cache, nearest fill, two triangles per cell.
- **DEC_r:**
  - pymeshlab `meshing_decimation_quadric_edge_collapse_with_texture(targetfacenum = REF/r, preserveboundary=True)`, other parameters default;
  - materials are kept apart by UV offsets (4 × 4 UV cells per material);
  - textures box-filtered by **32**, giving a texel of 32 × median ≈ 0.53–0.60 m (CBD) and 0.64–0.69 m (SUBURB).
- **WALL:**
  - quads on the shared cell edge where |Δh| > 2 m;
  - colour = mean of a 3 × 3 grid of horizontal rays per quad, starting 1.25 m behind the quad and cast outward (open3d), reading the full-resolution texel;
  - hits farther than 2.5 m outside the quad are ignored;
  - no hit → colour of the lower cell.

Notes on DEC:
- **Per tile:** the largest tile, D4-04, takes 5.2–5.8 min and 6.4–6.5 GB RSS for each r.
- **Hausdorff method:** one sample per REF face, area-uniform. Tiles are pooled with weight = tile area / samples. Its own cost was 3 min 47 s for all 54 jobs.
- **UV seams cap the reduction:** r = 4 and r = 16 hit the target. r = 64 stops at 2.39–2.60 × the target, at about 1 face per UV island (D4-04: 383 k faces for 641 k islands).
- **Texture stretch:** the texture-aware quadric still collapses across islands inside a material. The area whose texel grew more than 4× is 1 % (r = 4), 8–9 % (r = 16) and 54–57 % (r = 64); see `uv_stretch` per tile.
- **Faces with inconsistent UVs:** 670 / 368 / 824 faces (CBD) and 4 / 4 / 3,171 (SUBURB) had non-finite UVs, or UVs that drifted out of their material's cell. They were given the centre of one texture and counted.
- **Restart:** a first DEC run with 2 × 2 UV cells failed on 15 of 54 jobs (UVs drifting out of their cell). All 54 were rebuilt with 4 × 4 cells; only the rebuilt results are reported.

## Deviations and open points

1. **A.4:** the I3S node levels and triangle density per level were not measured within the 15 min budget. The services exist, and the metadata is saved.
2. **REF for CBD at ×8, not ×2:** ×2 needs about 25 GB of RGBA, and ×4 filled the card. SUBURB REF is at ×2. Downscaling REF from ×2 to ×8 changes the render by ≤ 0.66 DN mean (SUBURB), and from ×4 to ×8 by ≤ 0.48 DN (CBD).
3. **Two GPUs:** renders ran on both TITAN Xp. GPU 1 is the display GPU (309–311 MiB baseline), and peak − baseline is reported. The memory-only probe GRID_PIPELINE570 uses the run_v2 local frame; it is not used in C.3.
4. **DEC quality limits:** DEC_64 does not reach its target (UV seams), and DEC stretches some UVs across islands. Both are quantified above and not corrected.
5. **WALL coverage:** WALL was built (about 3 min in total). 13.5–14.0 % of its quads got no REF hit within ±1.25 m to +2.5 m and use the lower cell's colour.
